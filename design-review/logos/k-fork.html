Fork stem, two joins

The fork-stem K with the plane joined two ways. Merged: the fuselage runs into the K's joint and the plane has no tail, so the plane is the letter's upper arm. Separate: the whole plane, tail and all, floating on the diagonal. In both the left wing clears the stem.

Fork stem, plane merged

The plane's fuselage runs straight into the K's joint, so the plane is the letter's upper arm: body, wings and nose only, no tail. The left wing stays clear of the stem.
