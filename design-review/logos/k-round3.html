Monogram K, round three

Two critique rounds later. The first row is your pick, "plane lifted", with only the fixes that measurably help. The second row is the one new idea worth showing: the K's stem drawn as a fork, so the mark carries the meal as well as the plane. Each comes with a small cut for 16 and 24px, where the tail fin was only noise. The critics also asked for the plane to be fused into the K's arm as a single stroke; I built that and it loses the plane, so it is not here.
