Logo options for Kosher Travel

Eight marks, each shown at the sizes it will actually be used: 16 and 24 for the browser tab and phone home screen, 32 and 48 for the header badge, 96 for the upload zone and empty states. Each row shows the tile on a light and a dark page, the bare mark in a single color, and a mock browser tab.

The job: read as a plane at 16px first. Carry the kosher-meal idea at larger sizes second. Never read as a cross.
