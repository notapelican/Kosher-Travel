Monogram K, round two

The chosen mark after a critique. Two families: the plane-lifted layout with the detail fixes applied (one stroke weight, flat terminals, the plane's tail on the K's joint), and a fully fused construction where the plane is drawn as the K's arm in the same stroke. The fused family satisfies the critique's rule but loses the plane; it is here for comparison.
